# ScopeBrush Project Features (Pitch Ready)

Welcome to **ScopeBrush**, a premier marketplace designed to empower local artists and provide art enthusiasts with a seamless platform to discover and purchase original masterpieces. Below is a comprehensive list of features tailored for investors.

---

## 🎨 For Artists: Empowering Creativity
ScopeBrush provides artists with a professional toolkit to manage their business and reach a wider audience.

### 📤 Advanced Artwork Upload System
- **Rich Media Support**: Upload up to 3 high-resolution images per artwork.
- **Mandatory Video Verification**: Integrated video upload (max 50MB, 30s) to ensure authenticity and quality for buyers.
- **Smart Price Calculation**: Automates final cost based on base price, platform fees, and shipping charges.
- **Detailed Specifications**: Define material, actual/packed dimensions, and weight for accurate logistics.
- **Draft Preservation**: Local storage integration to save progress during the upload process.

### 📊 Comprehensive Artist Dashboard
- **Live Order Management**: Track sales through every stage (Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered).
- **Intelligent Pickup Scheduling**: Integrated tool for artists to schedule couriers (3-5 days in advance) with specific time slots.
- **Financial Analytics**: 
    - Track earnings per painting.
    - Monitor payment status (Successful/Pending).
    - Transparent transaction history with UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) tracking.
- **Profile Customization**: Dedicated artist profiles with sharing functionality to boost organic reach.

---

## 👤 For Users: Seamless Art Acquisition
A premium shopping experience designed for art lovers.

### 🔍 Art Discovery & Purchase
- **Curated Galleries**: Browse local artists and explore diverse categories (Portrait, Abstract, Digital, etc.).
- **Interactive Prototyping**: High-fidelity product views with zoom-in features and detailed artist biographies.
- **Secure Checkout**: Integrated with **Razorpay** for safe, multi-method payments.
- **Transparent Pricing**: Breakdown of taxes, discounts, and delivery fees at checkout.

### 📦 Order Tracking & Management
- **Real-time Status Updates**: Users can track their order progress from payment to delivery.
- **Buyer Protection**: 24-hour order confirmation window and integrated refund policies.
- **Feedback System**: Direct channel for users to provide feedback on their experience.

---

## 🛠️ Administrative & Operational Excellence
The "engine" that powers the marketplace.

### 🛡️ Admin Powerhouse (EFBV Role)
- **Real-time Analytics**: High-level time-series charts for user signups, artist registrations, and sales volume.
- **Order Control Center**: 
    - 24-hour safeguard timer before order confirmation.
    - Integrated tracking ID management and extra delivery charges handling.
- **Payment Verification**: Dedicated portal to verify artist payments via QR codes and UTR entry.
- **Platform Maintenance**: Manage app versions and force updates for the mobile APK directly from the dashboard.
- **Profit Tracking**: Automated calculation of platform earnings (Gross Amount - Costs - Commissions).

---

## 📦 Order Management Flow: The Lifecycle
1. **Transaction**: Buyer pays via Razorpay → Order marked as **Paid**.
2. **Verification**: Admin monitors order → 24-hour timer expires → Marked as **Confirmed**.
3. **Logistics**: Artist sets **Pickup Schedule** → Order marked as **Shipped** (Tracking ID added).
4. **Fulfillment**: Courier delivers artwork → Marked as **Delivered**.
5. **Settlement**: Platform processes **Artist Payment** → Artist receives UTR verification.

## 📱 Technical Edge & Rich Aesthetics
- **Cross-Platform**: Built with **React** and **Capacitor** for a native Android/iOS experience.
- **Premium UI/UX**: Features glassmorphism, smooth animations (Framer Motion), and a "Mobile-First" responsive design.
- **Global Reach**: SEO optimized with automated sitemaps and deep linking for seamless app navigation.
- **Push Notifications**: Integrated notification system for order updates and new features.
